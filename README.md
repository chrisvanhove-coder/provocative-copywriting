# Provocative LinkedIn Copy

A Claude skill for writing LinkedIn posts that people read to the last line.

I built it for my own posts about fragrance, language and perception. This public version has everything specific to my business taken out, so you can fill in your own voice and use it for any field.

## What it does

- Asks only for what's missing: the idea, a heat level from 1 to 5, the goal (leads, reach or authority) and any proof you have.
- Writes five hooks from different families and recommends one.
- Builds the post as a five-act play with a turn in the middle.
- Edits sentences with newsroom rules and filters out AI-sounding phrasing.
- Applies LinkedIn mechanics from an analysis of 1.2 million posts: length, hashtags, links, visuals.
- Gives you the hook one level hotter and one level cooler, so you can choose how bold to be.
- Also critiques drafts, writes hooks only, and drafts comments on other people's posts.

## The ideas behind it

**Hooks.** Joseph Sugarman's "slippery slide": the only job of the first sentence is to get the second one read. The data agrees with him: first lines that are statements beat questions, numbers help, and short lines win.

**Shakespeare.** Gustav Freytag's five-act pyramid, compressed to 300 words. The turn (volta) of a Shakespearean sonnet. Mark Antony's funeral speech in *Julius Caesar*, which demolishes his opponents without a single insult.

**Journalism.** The 25-word test, news values, the nut graf, Roy Peter Clark's *Writing Tools* and George Orwell's six rules.

**The heat dial.** Research by Zoey Chen and Jonah Berger shows controversy increases discussion only up to a moderate level. Beyond that, discomfort wins and people stop talking. On a platform where everyone posts under their real name, the sweet spot is usually 2 to 4.

## How to use it

1. Download the `provocative-linkedin-copy` folder.
2. Open `SKILL.md` and fill in section 0 with your voice and brand. If you share your filled-in version, leave out anything private.
3. Add the folder to Claude as a custom skill. See Anthropic's documentation for the current steps.
4. Ask things like:
   - "Write a LinkedIn post about [your idea]. Heat 3, goal: authority."
   - "Give me 10 hooks for this draft: [paste]"
   - "Critique this post before I publish it."
   - "Write a comment on this post: [paste]"
